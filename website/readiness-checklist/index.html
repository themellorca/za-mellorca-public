What your score means

  • 17 to 20: a strong foundation. Automation is likely to pay off quickly, as long as each automation has an owner.
  • 11 to 16: some gaps worth fixing first. Automating now may speed up the problems as well as the work.
  • 0 to 10: your systems are probably costing time every week. A diagnosis will show which few changes matter most.
