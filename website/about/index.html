About Mellorca

Engineering the digital operating layer of modern businesses.

Mellorca exists to make the systems businesses rely on easier to operate, connect, automate and improve.

What Mellorca is

Mellorca is a digital systems and operations company working across Digital Infrastructure, Digital Operations and Business Automation.

The company sits between strategy and implementation. We are interested in what the business needs to achieve, how work actually moves, what systems support that work and what should be automated—or deliberately kept human.

How we think about the work

  • Diagnose before prescribing.
  • Simplify before automating.
  • Integrate before duplicating.
  • Prefer maintainable systems over clever systems.
  • Document what is built.
  • Use AI where it has a justified operational role.

Who leads Mellorca

Mellorca is led by Qwaname Kenobisan, who makes sure every engagement starts with how your business actually works, not with a tool, and ends with systems your team can understand and run.

Platform philosophy

Mellorca is platform-agnostic where practical. The business problem should determine the tool, not the other way around. Existing systems should not be replaced simply because a different platform is fashionable.

What we are not trying to be

Mellorca is not positioned as a generic IT helpdesk, marketing agency, CRM-only consultancy, no-code automation freelancer, AI agency or cybersecurity company without the corresponding specialist capability.

Company positioning

Mellorca designs, improves and automates the digital systems businesses depend on to operate.

If disconnected systems, manual workarounds or unreliable automation are holding your business back, that is the work we do.
