About Mellorca

We make the systems your business runs on easier to operate, connect and improve.

Mellorca is a digital systems and operations company. We work with owners, executives and operations leaders when disconnected tools, manual workarounds or unreliable automation start to hold the business back.

What Mellorca does

We work across three connected areas:

  • Digital Infrastructure: the platforms, accounts, data and integrations your operations depend on.
  • Digital Operations: how work actually moves between people, teams and systems.
  • Business Automation: automating repeatable work, with clear controls for the parts that should stay human.

We sit between strategy and implementation. We start with what the business needs to achieve and how work really happens today, then decide what to fix, what to connect, what to automate and what to leave alone.

Who we work with

Our clients are usually established businesses where the systems grew faster than the structure around them. Typical signs: the same data typed into several tools, reports rebuilt by hand each month, automations nobody fully understands, and one or two people who hold critical knowledge in their heads.

The people we talk to first are owners, managing directors, operations leaders and finance leads. They know something is costing time and money, and want a clear, honest view of what to do about it.

How we think about the work

  • Diagnose before prescribing. We find out what is actually broken before recommending anything.
  • Simplify before automating. Automating a confused process only makes the confusion faster.
  • Integrate before duplicating. Connect the tools you have before adding new ones.
  • Maintainable over clever. Your team should be able to understand and run what we build.
  • Document everything. Every engagement ends with written records of what was changed and why.
  • AI where it earns its place. We use AI when it has a clear operational job and the right controls.

What you can expect from us

  • A written scope before any work starts, so you know what will be delivered.
  • Plain language. We explain trade-offs without jargon.
  • Your systems stay yours. Access, accounts and documentation are handed back at the end.
  • Honest advice, including when the right answer is to change less.

Platform choices

We are not tied to any vendor. The business problem decides the tool, not the other way around, and we will not suggest replacing a system just because a different platform is fashionable.

What we do not do

Mellorca is not a general IT helpdesk, a marketing agency or a single-product consultancy. Where a project needs specialist capability we do not have, such as dedicated cybersecurity testing, we say so.
