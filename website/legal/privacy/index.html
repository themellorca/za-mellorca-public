Privacy

What information Mellorca collects through this website, why we collect it and how you can ask us about it.

Information you choose to share

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you enter: your name, work email, organisation, website or system context, industry, organisation size, role, how you found us, the service you are interested in and your description of the problem.

When you sign up for The Mellorca Briefing, we collect your email address, your industry, your role if you give it, and the page you signed up from.

How we use it

We use enquiry details to understand your situation, reply to you and suggest the right starting point. We use briefing details to send you the briefing and to choose articles that are relevant to readers in your industry and role. We do not sell your information.

Service providers

Like most websites, we rely on providers for hosting, form delivery, email and website analytics. They process information only to provide those services to us.

Analytics

We use website analytics to understand which pages are useful and how visitors find us, so we can improve what we publish.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the briefing at any time using the link in any email. You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to us through the contact page.

Security

Please do not send passwords, API keys, payment credentials or other system secrets through any form on this website.
