Draft policy

Privacy

This page is a working policy draft maintained as part of Mellorca's core static pages and requires legal review before production publication.

Production blocker: confirm Mellorca's legal entity, jurisdiction, privacy contact, retention rules, analytics stack, CRM/webhook destination and applicable regulatory obligations.

Information submitted directly

The website may collect information a visitor chooses to submit, such as name, work email, organisation, website or system context, enquiry intent and a description of the business problem or requirement.

How it may be used

Submitted information may be used to respond to enquiries, assess fit, route the request to the appropriate Mellorca service, maintain business records and improve legitimate service-delivery workflows.

Service providers

Mellorca may use hosting, form-processing, CRM, email, analytics and related operational service providers. The production policy must identify or appropriately describe the actual categories and processing practices in use.

Retention and rights

Retention periods and user rights depend on the applicable jurisdiction and real operating requirements. These must be finalised before this draft is treated as a production privacy policy.

Contact

A verified privacy contact address must be inserted before production publication.
