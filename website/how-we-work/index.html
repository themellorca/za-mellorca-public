How we work

A clear way of working, from first conversation to systems that run.

The engagement path

Five stages. You can start at whichever one fits.

Some businesses need a diagnosis first. Others already know what must be built. The stages connect, but you only buy the ones you need.

01 · Assess

Understand what is really happening

We map your systems, workflows, handoffs and data flows, and talk to the people who use them. You get a clear picture of where time, money and risk are being lost, ranked by what matters most.

02 · Design

Agree the target and the route

We design how systems, data and workflows should fit together, and sequence the work so value arrives early. You get an architecture and roadmap your team can understand and challenge.

03 · Build

Implement and connect

We configure, integrate, migrate and test against the agreed design. Everything we build is documented as we go, not reconstructed at the end.

04 · Improve

Remove friction and automate the right work

We simplify processes before automating them, then automate the repeatable work, with clear ownership, monitoring and a way to handle exceptions.

05 · Operate

Keep it healthy

For systems you want us to look after, we monitor, maintain and keep improving them under an agreed scope, so they do not decay once the project team moves on.

Not sure where to start?

Begin with a diagnosis

When the symptoms are clear but the cause is not, the Digital Systems Audit is the safest first step. It ends with a ranked plan, whether or not we do the rest.

From first conversation to signed scope

  1. Tell us what is getting in the way. Use the contact form and describe it in your own words. No technical brief is needed.
  2. A focused discovery call. We ask about your systems, people, constraints and what a good outcome looks like, and tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.
  3. A written proposal. It sets out the problem as we understand it, the scope, deliverables, responsibilities on both sides, timeline and fees. Pricing is specific to each engagement, so we discuss it once the scope is understood, never before.
  4. Agreement, then kick-off. We confirm owners, access, communication rhythm and how progress will be reported, then begin.

What working with us is like

  • One accountable lead for your engagement, with direct access throughout.
  • Regular, written progress updates that show what was done, what is next and any decision we need from you.
  • Decisions made with you, not for you. We explain trade-offs in plain language before anything important changes.
  • Minimal disruption. We work around your operations and plan changes so the business keeps running.

Access and security

We request system access only when the work needs it, limit it to that work, and ask for it to be removed when we are done. We never ask for passwords or keys by email or web form. Read more in our data handling notes.

Handover you can rely on

At the end of a build or improvement project you receive the documentation, owners and runbooks needed to operate what we built, whether your team runs it or we do. Nothing should depend on one person remembering how it works.

In short

Diagnose before prescribing. Simplify before automating. Document what is built. Hand over properly.
