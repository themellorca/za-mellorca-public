Automation principle

Do not automate a broken process merely to make the broken process run faster.

Mellorca simplifies or corrects process logic where necessary, then automates the parts that benefit from automation.

AI is optional

A rules-based integration may be more reliable, cheaper and easier to support than an AI workflow. AI is used when it improves the operating result, not to make the project sound current.

Operational control

Reliable automation includes ownership, monitoring, error handling, exception paths and documentation—not just a successful happy-path demo.
