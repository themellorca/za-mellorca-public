The Digital Systems Audit is a structured paid diagnostic for businesses that can feel operational friction but do not yet have a reliable diagnosis of the systems, workflow or information-flow problem underneath it.

Start a Digital Systems Audit

Best fit

This is useful when systems have accumulated, work is too manual, handoffs are unreliable, reporting is weak or automation has become fragile—but the business is not yet sure what should be fixed first.

What Mellorca assesses

  • Digital systems and platforms currently in use
  • Core workflows and operational handoffs
  • Integrations and information movement
  • Manual dependencies and repetitive work
  • CRM and operational tooling where relevant
  • Reporting and decision visibility
  • Existing automation and AI use
  • Ownership, dependencies and unnecessary complexity

Core outputs

  • Current-state systems and workflow view
  • Key bottlenecks and failure points
  • Integration and information-flow observations
  • Manual-work and automation opportunities
  • Priority risks and dependencies
  • Prioritised roadmap
  • Recommendations on what to keep, fix, integrate, automate, replace or leave alone

What this is not

This is not a statutory, financial, compliance or cybersecurity audit. Those disciplines require different specialist scopes and qualifications.

Engagement outcome

You should leave with a clearer picture of the operating environment, a ranked set of problems and a rational next step—whether that next step involves Mellorca or not.
