Automate repeatable work without automating the confusion.

Business Automation coordinates actions across people, systems and information. Mellorca uses workflow automation and AI where they create operational value—not because every process needs more technology.

Discuss an automation project

Capability scope

  • Workflow and system-to-system automation
  • Data synchronisation
  • Routing, notifications and approvals
  • CRM and operational automation
  • Automated reporting and document workflows
  • AI-assisted operational workflows
  • AI agents where the use case, controls and economics justify them
  • Human approval and exception paths
  • Automation monitoring, error handling and maintenance
Do not automate a broken process merely to make the broken process run faster.

AI has a job, not an identity

AI is one tool inside the automation capability. A deterministic workflow, better configuration or clearer ownership may be the better answer. The technology should follow the operating requirement.

Maintainability matters

Automations need ownership, observability, exception handling and documentation. A clever workflow nobody can support is a future failure point.
