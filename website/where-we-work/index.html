Where we work

Your systems are not in one place. Neither is our work.

Mellorca works remotely with businesses across countries and time zones. The platforms, integrations and workflows we work on are the same wherever your teams sit, and so is the way we deliver.

Talk to MellorcaHow we work
Remote by design

Built for teams in different places.

Most systems work happens inside cloud platforms, business applications and integrations, so it can be done well remotely when it is organised properly.

Overlap with your working day

We agree regular working hours that overlap with your team, and schedule workshops, cut-overs and go-lives at times that suit your operation.

Written by default

Decisions, progress and documentation are written down and shared, so people in different time zones always know where things stand.

On site when it matters

Some moments, such as discovery workshops or a major go-live, benefit from being in the room. Where that is the case, we agree it as part of the scope.

Working across borders

Businesses with operations in more than one country face extra questions. We plan for them from the start rather than discovering them mid-project.

Data location and privacy

Where your data is stored and processed can matter for regulation and for your customers. We identify these requirements during assessment and design systems and integrations that respect them.

Multi-entity and multi-currency operations

Group structures, regional entities, currencies, tax settings and local reporting needs are a common source of disconnected systems. We design for them explicitly.

Different rules, one operating model

Where teams in different countries follow different processes, we help you decide what should be standard everywhere and what genuinely needs to differ locally.

Contracts and invoicing

Scope, responsibilities, currency and invoicing arrangements are set out in the proposal and agreement for each engagement, so both sides know exactly how cross-border work will run.

Language

We work in English. Documentation, workshops and handover material are written in clear, plain English that non-native speakers can follow.

Planning work in another country?

Tell us where your teams and systems are, and what is getting in the way. We will tell you how we would approach it.
