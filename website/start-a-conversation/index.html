Start a conversation

What happens after you get in touch.

Talking to Mellorca should feel simple and low-risk. Here is exactly what happens from your first message to an agreed scope.

Talk to Mellorca

Five steps

  1. You write to us. Use the contact form. Describe what is happening in your own words; it takes a few minutes and you do not need technical detail.
  2. A person reads it. Every message is read by a person, not routed into an automated sales sequence. If something is unclear we reply by email with a few questions.
  3. A first conversation. We arrange a call to understand your business, what is going wrong and what you have already tried. No preparation is needed and there is no obligation.
  4. A suggested starting point. Usually one of three things: a Digital Systems Audit when the cause is unclear, a defined project when the scope is already understood, or honest advice that you do not need us.
  5. Scope in writing. If we go ahead, you receive a written scope that sets out what will be delivered, how and by when. Pricing is discussed at this stage, once the work is understood. Nothing starts until you agree it.

What helps in your first message

  • What is going wrong, and how often.
  • Which systems or tools are involved, if you know.
  • Who is affected: customers, staff, finance, management.
  • What you have already tried.

What not to send

Never send passwords, API keys, private keys or payment details through the form or by email. If access is needed later, we agree a secure way to handle it as part of the scope.

Common questions

Will I be added to a mailing list?

Only if you tick the briefing box on the form. Otherwise we use your details to reply to you and nothing else.

Do I need to know what the problem is?

No. Many conversations start with "something is not working and we are not sure why". Finding the cause is part of our work.

Is the first conversation a sales pitch?

No. It is a working conversation about your business. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.

Can you work with the systems we already have?

Yes. We are not tied to any vendor, and we prefer to improve and connect what you have before suggesting anything new.

Ready when you are

Tell us what is getting in the way. A short message is enough to start.
